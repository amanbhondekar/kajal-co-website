Our Works / SEO / Udaan Travel
Travel · SEO

Organic bookings up 3.9× in seven months

Turning a tour operator’s itinerary pages into the destination research people actually search for.

Client
Udaan Travel
Industry
Tour operator
Service
Technical SEO, content
Market
India · outbound
Duration
7 months
01 · The challenge

Competing with aggregators on their own terms

Udaan’s site was a list of itineraries with prices. It competed directly with aggregators on booking queries, lost, and had nothing to offer the much larger research audience arriving earlier in the journey.

  • Itinerary pages were the only content, and all of them targeted booking queries
  • No content addressed visa, season, cost or packing questions
  • Thin itinerary descriptions were duplicated across similar trips
  • Paid search was the only reliable source of bookings
02 · SEO strategy

Win the research, then route it

Nobody books a trip on the first search. We built for the questions that come months earlier and connected each one to the itinerary it leads to.

01

Destination clusters

Each destination became a cluster: a guide, the practical questions, the seasonal advice, and the itineraries that serve it.

02

Research intent

Visa, best-season, budget and safety questions were treated as primary content rather than as blog filler.

03

Itinerary differentiation

Rewrote 60 itineraries so similar trips no longer competed with each other on near-identical copy.

04

Internal routing

Every guide ends with the itineraries it implies, so research traffic has a commercial next step.

03 · What we changed

The work, in four buckets.

01

Technical SEO

Consolidated the duplicate itinerary variants that were splitting authority, and fixed a canonical pattern that had seasonal pages competing with their own parents.

02

Content

Built eleven destination clusters, each with a guide, four to six practical questions and the itineraries that serve it.

03

On-page SEO

Rewrote 60 itinerary pages so each targets a distinct trip type rather than a shared destination keyword.

04

Site structure

Routed every guide into its matching itineraries, turning research sessions into a measurable booking path.

05 · Business impact

Reached earlier, at a lower cost

Meeting travellers at the research stage put Udaan in front of them months before an aggregator would have, and at no marginal cost per visit.

Research traffic converts more slowly than booking traffic but far more cheaply, which has let the operator reduce paid search on the destinations where the clusters are strongest.

The guides also give the sales team something to send during a long consideration period, which it did not previously have.
