Beauty & Personal Care · Performance Marketing

E-Commerce Catalog Scaling & Full-Funnel Paid Social Acquisition

Scaling direct-to-consumer beauty and personal care e-commerce through structured Meta Ads prospecting, dynamic catalog retargeting, and bundle promotion.

Client
KishKin
Industry
Beauty & Personal Care
Channels
Meta Ads, Shopify, Google Analytics
Duration
8 months
Objective
Scale D2C beauty orders and maximize customer lifetime value
Location
India
01 · The challenge

Rising customer acquisition costs and low repeat purchase rates in beauty

The D2C beauty and personal care landscape in India is fiercely competitive. KishKin faced escalating ad costs and struggled to convert initial product interest into sustainable purchases.

  • Ad spend was spread thinly across dozens of individual SKUs without clear best-seller focus.
  • High percentage of one-off impulse purchasers with low average order value (AOV).
  • Retargeting lacked dynamism, showing generic brand ads rather than exact products viewed.
02 · Campaign strategy

Hero SKU acquisition, routine bundles, and dynamic catalog feeds

We concentrated top-of-funnel ad budget on 3 validated hero products, using routine bundles to drive higher basket sizes and dynamic catalog ads to capture warm abandoners.

Audience
Women and men aged 18–40 interested in clean skincare, beauty routines, and dermatological care
Offer
Curated 3-step routine bundles with complimentary trial gifts and free shipping
Creative
UGC product texture applications, before-and-after routine carousels, and founder formulation spotlights
Meta Ads (Facebook & Instagram Feed, Stories, Reels)Shopify Product CatalogGA4
Step 01 Hero SKU Prospecting

Video ads demonstrating product application, ingredient benefits, and real customer skin transformations.

Step 02 Bundle Consideration

Carousel ads showing complete morning and evening skincare regimens to increase order value.

Step 03 Dynamic Catalog Retargeting

Automated DPA ads displaying exact browsed products to cart abandoners within 7 days.

Step 04 Post-Purchase Nurturing

Cross-sell campaigns offering complementary treatments to existing customers at day 30.

03 · Execution

What actually went live.

01

Hero product consolidation

Reallocated 70% of cold prospecting budget to the top 3 best-selling formulations, creating strong algorithmic learning signals.

02

Dynamic Product Ads (DPA) integration

Connected a real-time Shopify catalog feed with custom frame overlays highlighting customer ratings and free shipping.

03

UGC and creator content testing

Ran continuous creative testing across customer review montages, unboxing clips, and texture close-ups.

04

Landing page routine bundler

Directed ad traffic to dedicated routine landing pages offering single-click complete kit additions.

04 · Optimisation

Decision, reason, outcome.

Decision

Shifted focus from single items to 3-step routine sets

Reason

Single items struggled to cover acquisition cost on standalone margins.

Outcome

Higher average basket size, improving unit economics across cold ad sets.

Decision

Implemented 7-day exclusion windows on retargeting

Reason

Bombarding recent buyers with the same product wasted budget and caused customer annoyance.

Outcome

Better ad frequency control and cleaner allocation toward prospective buyers.

Decision

Replaced studio beauty shots with raw phone-recorded texture clips

Reason

Overly polished commercial ads were scrolled past; native-feeling creator video stopped thumbs.

Outcome

Higher video hook rate and lower cost per outbound click.

06 · Business result

Scalable e-commerce acquisition framework

The restructured campaign framework created an efficient acquisition engine supporting both new customer growth and catalog discovery.

Built an agile creative pipeline that generated fresh ad concepts every fortnight.

Established dynamic catalog ads as the highest-converting remarketing channel for store visitors.

Key takeaway

In e-commerce beauty, profit lives in the bundle and the repeat purchase. Focusing cold spend on proven hero items while using dynamic catalog ads for remarketing stabilized customer acquisition efficiency.
