Our Works / Website Design / Sahana Academy
Education · Website Design

Course applications up 2.8×

Redesigning an education brand’s digital experience to simplify course discovery and increase applications.

Client
Sahana Academy
Industry
Professional education
Services
IA, UX, visual design, build
Platform
WordPress
Timeline
8 weeks
01 · The challenge

A homepage about the institute, not the courses

Sahana’s 34 courses were reachable only through a dropdown menu three levels deep. The homepage described the institute’s history to an audience that had arrived to compare syllabuses and fees.

Prospective students could not filter by format, duration or eligibility without reading every page.

The admissions team spent most of its phone time answering questions the site already contained but did not surface.

Let a visitor find the right course and start an application in one session.

02 · The strategy

Make discovery the primary journey

The homepage was restructured around the information a prospective student needs before choosing a course, rather than treating it as a brand showcase.

Course finder on the homepage
Fees, eligibility and dates on every course page
One application form across all courses
Outcomes shown with the course, not on a separate page
03 · The experience

The decisions that did the work.

Decision 01

A course finder, not a menu

Visitors filter by field, format, duration and start date, and see matching courses with fees and eligibility on the card itself.

Why it mattered

It replaced a navigation problem with a comparison tool, which is what people were trying to do anyway.

Decision 02

Every course page answers the same nine questions

Fees, duration, eligibility, start dates, syllabus, faculty, assessment, outcomes and the application deadline, in that order, on every course.

Why it mattered

Consistency makes courses comparable. It also removed most of the repeat questions reaching the admissions desk.

05 · Implementation

Courses as structured content

Each course is a structured record rather than a hand-built page, so the finder, the comparison view and the course page all read from the same fields.

  • Custom course type with required fields for fees, dates and eligibility
  • Finder filters generated from the course data, not maintained separately
  • One application form, pre-filled with the course being applied to
  • Admissions team can open and close intakes without editing pages
