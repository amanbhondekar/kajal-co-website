E-Commerce Search Architecture & GA4-Tracked Organic Scaling

A data-driven technical SEO and e-commerce category overhaul delivering 4.4K tracked users, 789 organic search sessions, and 46 direct online purchases for a UK luxury furniture retailer.

01 · The challenge

E-commerce collection cannibalization and index bloat on Shopify

Blythe Living offers high-ticket garden furniture, fire pit sets, and aluminum pergolas across the UK. Organic traffic was stagnant due to unstructured Shopify tag URLs and missing commercial category optimization.

  • Shopify’s native collection tag filtering generated hundreds of duplicate, thin URLs indexed by search engines.
  • Category pages lacked substantive commercial copy, leaving the site unable to compete against national UK furniture chains.
  • Product pages carried missing or duplicate meta titles and incomplete structured product data.
02 · SEO strategy

Commercial category restructuring and crawl optimization

We eliminated index bloat by enforcing strict canonical rules, enriched category pages with buyer guides, and implemented full e-commerce schema.

01

Crawl & Index Cleanup

Canonicalized automated tag URLs and updated robots.txt rules to focus Google’s crawl budget exclusively on master commercial collections.

02

Commercial Category Architecture

Restructured collections into high-demand search clusters (Rattan Garden Furniture, Aluminium Pergolas, Fire Pit Dining Sets) with 300–500 words of expert buying guidance.

03

Product Schema Enrichment

Deployed comprehensive Product, AggregateRating, and Offer schema to qualify product listings for Google Rich Snippets in UK search results.

04

Internal Link Equity Distribution

Connected blog buying guides directly into high-value commercial categories, channeling informational search traffic to transactional shelves.

03 · What we changed

The work, in four buckets.

01

Technical Shopify SEO

Resolved collection duplicate URL canonicalization, eliminated 404 broken links, and accelerated mobile loading speeds.

02

Commercial On-Page Optimization

Rewrote title tags, meta descriptions, and semantic headings across 40+ key furniture collections and top-selling SKUs.

03

Buying Guides & Informational Content

Published comprehensive seasonal furniture care and measurement guides answering specific pre-purchase search queries.

04

GA4 Analytics & Attribution Setup

Configured verified GA4 e-commerce tracking to measure organic search session pathways through to final cart checkout.
