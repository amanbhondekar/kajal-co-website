Organic Search Acquisition & GA4-Verified E-Commerce Scaling

Technical SEO cleanup, category keyword architecture, and structured e-commerce optimization driving 17K tracked users, 3.0K organic sessions, and 28 direct online purchases.

01 · The challenge

High dependency on paid channels with negligible organic presence

KishKin Digital operates an expansive e-commerce storefront. With customer acquisition costs climbing, the business needed organic search to become a dependable contributor to online orders.

  • E-commerce catalog suffered from duplicate titles and missing image alt descriptions across hundreds of SKUs.
  • Collection pages carried no descriptive copy, resulting in zero top-page rankings for commercial category terms.
  • Site suffered from mobile speed bottlenecks due to uncompressed images and bloated JavaScript apps.
02 · SEO strategy

Catalog cleanliness, commercial intent keywords, and speed optimization

We focused on high-intent category search queries, optimized product page metadata at scale, and resolved core web vitals bottlenecks.

01

Technical Infrastructure & Core Web Vitals

Compressed asset delivery, deferred non-critical tracking scripts, and improved Largest Contentful Paint (LCP).

02

Commercial Intent Category Pages

Targeted high-volume commercial keywords with unique category descriptions, FAQs, and semantic heading hierarchies.

03

Scalable Product Metadata Optimization

Created programmatic title and meta description templates ensuring 100% unique metadata across all catalog items.

04

Structured Product & Offer Schema

Implemented rich snippet schema markup to display prices, stock availability, and ratings in search engine results.

03 · What we changed

The work, in four buckets.

01

Technical SEO & Page Speed

Resolved crawl errors, eliminated duplicate canonical tags, and brought mobile load speeds within recommended Core Web Vitals thresholds.

02

On-Page Category Optimization

Wrote targeted copy and optimized meta data across all core product category and collection pages.

03

Structured Data Implementation

Deployed Product, BreadcrumbList, and Organization schema across the entire e-commerce catalog.

04

GA4 Event Tracking & Attribution

Configured robust GA4 tracking to verify user acquisition channels and e-commerce transactions.
