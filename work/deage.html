DeAge

Clinical Credibility & Consultation Experience for Anti-Aging Science

A precision-crafted digital presence for a premier Mumbai anti-aging clinic, turning complex dermatological science into trusted client bookings.

Industry
Health & Wellness
Service
UX/UI Design & Shopify Development
Location
Mumbai, India
Duration
3 months
01 · The challenge

Advanced anti-aging science requiring accessible consumer trust

DeAge delivers advanced aesthetic dermatology, longevity treatments, and clinical skincare. High-ticket clinical procedures require immense patient trust, which their previous presence struggled to convey.

Patients felt intimidated by technical medical jargon without clear explanations of treatment expectations and downtime.

No clear distinction between in-clinic medical procedures and home-care clinical skincare formulations.

High first-contact drop-off due to an ambiguous consultation booking process.

Create an authoritative, luxurious digital presence that articulates advanced dermatological science clearly and drives qualified consultation bookings.

02 · The strategy

Clinical authority paired with luxury aesthetic clarity

We developed an editorial clinical visual system that explains anti-aging treatments transparently, showcases medical qualifications, and provides intuitive booking funnels.

01Minimalist, clinical-luxury design language inspired by modern dermatological institutes
02Treatment transparency detailing procedure steps, candidate suitability, and recovery time
03Bifurcated funnel distinguishing in-clinic clinical consultations from curated home formulations
04Direct consultation calendar and WhatsApp inquiry pathways
03 · The experience

The decisions that did the work.

Decision 01

Procedure expectation cards with recovery transparency

Each clinical service page includes structured expectation panels detailing procedure duration, sensation, downtime, and expected results timeline.

Why it mattered

Demystifying the clinical experience removes anxiety and pre-qualifies patients before consultation calls.

Decision 02

Physician credentials and clinical technology showcase

Spotlighted US-FDA-cleared technologies, sterile clinical protocols, and specialist medical leadership.

Why it mattered

In aesthetic medicine, patient confidence hinges entirely on proven medical authority and technology safety.

Decision 03

Integrated clinical skincare e-commerce path

Designed a dedicated Shopify-powered catalog for physician-curated skincare products alongside clinic services.

Why it mattered

Creates post-treatment skincare regimen continuity and opens scalable direct product revenue.

Precision dermatological system

Modern monochrome clinical foundation accented with refined metallic undertones.

05 · Implementation

Figma to Shopify & Web engineering

Iterative Figma design system transitioned into a custom responsive frontend with fast load performance.

  • Complete end-to-end Figma UI component library with dark/light clinical states
  • Custom Shopify theme integration powering both service inquiry and product checkout
  • Direct consultation routing into the clinic reception booking workflow
  • Fast sub-2 second mobile performance across 4G and broadband connections
