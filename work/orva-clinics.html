Healthcare · Website Design

Online bookings up 4.3× across six locations

Replacing six inconsistent clinic sites with one system that lets patients find the nearest location and book without calling.

Client
Orva Clinics
Industry
Primary care · Multi-location
Services
IA, UX, design system, build
Platform
Next.js + headless CMS
Timeline
10 weeks
01 · The challenge

Six clinics, six websites, one confused patient

Each Orva location had grown its own site with its own hours, its own services list and its own phone number. Patients routinely arrived at the wrong clinic or called a location that did not offer the service they needed.

Opening hours and service lists contradicted each other across locations.

Booking meant calling during business hours, which is exactly when most patients are at work.

One accurate source of clinic information, and a booking a patient can complete on a phone.

02 · The strategy

One system, six front doors

Patients do not think in locations until they need one. The site leads with the service and resolves the location afterwards.

Search by service, then by proximity
Hours and services managed once, per clinic
Booking completable on a phone in under a minute
Accessibility treated as a requirement, not a review step
03 · The experience

The decisions that did the work.

Decision 01

Service first, location second

A patient picks what they need, then sees which clinics offer it, how far each is and the next available slot at each.

Why it mattered

It matches how patients actually decide, and it stopped the misdirected visits that were costing reception staff hours each week.

Decision 02

Clinic data entered once

Hours, services, clinicians and holiday closures live on one record per clinic and render everywhere that clinic appears.

Why it mattered

The contradictions were not a design problem, they were a data problem. One record per clinic removed the whole class of error.

Decision 03

A booking flow built for one hand

Three steps, large targets, no account required, and a confirmation by SMS rather than email.

Why it mattered

Most bookings happen on a phone, often one-handed and often in a hurry. The flow was built for that case rather than adapted to it.

04 · The design system

A system six clinics can share

Components built so a new location can launch without design work, and so accessibility holds without per-page review.

Typography
16px minimum body, 4.5:1 contrast enforced in tokens
Components
Clinic card, service card, slot picker, hours table
Accessibility
WCAG 2.2 AA, keyboard-complete booking flow
Scale
A seventh clinic is a content record, not a build
05 · Implementation

Static pages, live availability

Clinic and service pages are statically rendered for speed; availability is fetched live from the practice-management system at the point of booking.

  • Clinic records drive pages, finder results and structured data
  • Live slot availability via the existing practice-management API
  • SMS confirmation and reminder on every booking
  • Local business structured data generated per clinic

Six sites were not six design problems. Consolidating clinic information into one record per location fixed the contradictions, and the booking flow could then be built once and trusted everywhere.
