01 · The challenge

Customer skepticism and high friction in digital growth service acquisition

Providing social media promotion and digital growth solutions is fraught with customer trust challenges, platform compliance risks, and price-sensitive churn.

  • Prospective clients frequently worried about service reliability, account security, and delivery speed.
  • Ad accounts faced compliance hurdles requiring careful ad copy navigation of platform terms.
  • High customer drop-off when transitioning from ad click to service selection.
02 · Campaign strategy

Trust-first direct response and transparent service tiers

We designed compliant ad creative emphasizing organic engagement strategies, client proof, and password-free delivery guarantees.

Audience
Digital creators, emerging entrepreneurs, and small business owners aged 20–45 seeking audience expansion
Offer
Guaranteed password-free service, 24/7 client support, and modular transparent service packages

04 · Optimisation

Decision, reason, outcome.

Decision

Emphasized "No Password Required" in primary ad headlines

Reason

Account security was the single largest objection stopping prospective buyers.

Outcome

Lowered click-to-inquiry hesitation immediately.

Decision

Paused generic audience targeting in favor of small business owners

Reason

Casual users generated low lifetime value compared to business profiles seeking consistent growth.

Outcome

Higher average transaction value and better customer retention.

Decision

Introduced transparent video screen demonstrations

Reason

Static text ads failed to build credibility; showing real dashboards built instant trust.

Outcome

Improved ad engagement and click-through rates.
