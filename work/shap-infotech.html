A modern, high-performance web experience positioning a Bangalore software services provider for enterprise IT contracts and technology partnerships.

01 · The challenge

Sophisticated technical capabilities obscured by generic software messaging

Shap Infotech provides enterprise cloud migration, bespoke software development, and digital transformation consulting. Their existing website sounded generic and failed to articulate specific technology capabilities to enterprise CTOs.

Technical capabilities across cloud infrastructure, DevOps, and custom development were grouped without clear delivery frameworks.

Enterprise decision-makers could not easily evaluate tech stacks, engagement models, or past case studies.

The mobile browsing experience suffered from text-heavy pages and slow loading performance.

Redesign and develop a sharp, modern B2B technology website in Figma and WordPress that articulates engineering maturity and captures enterprise RFP leads.

02 · The strategy

Engineering maturity meets modular capability discovery

We architected a technology matrix highlighting proven tech stacks, agile delivery methodologies, and clear engagement tiers (Dedicated Teams, Project Basis, Managed Services).

01Modern dark/light tech aesthetic featuring high-tech indigo accents and clean line geometry
02Modular capability hubs mapping services directly to enterprise business outcomes
03Technology stack filters showcasing proficiencies (AWS, React, Node, Python, Docker)
04Enterprise RFP intake flow capturing project scope, budget range, and timeline
03 · The experience

The decisions that did the work.

Decision 01

Technology stack matrix with proficiency indicators

Built an interactive capabilities grid showcasing frameworks, cloud environments, and databases deployed across projects.

Why it mattered

Engineering leaders vetting agency partners look for immediate proof of specific stack proficiency.

Decision 02

Transparent engagement model framework

Created dedicated breakdowns explaining Dedicated Teams, Fixed Price SOWs, and Staff Augmentation models.

Why it mattered

Clarifies commercial working structures upfront and shortens the sales discovery cycle.

Decision 03

Technical inquiry brief with file upload capabilities

Replaced single-line contact forms with an RFP workflow allowing prospective clients to submit requirement documents.

Why it mattered

Delivers qualified briefs directly to the solution architecture team for faster turnaround on technical proposals.

05 · Implementation

Figma prototypes into custom WordPress build

Designed in Figma with reusable components and coded as a fast, maintainable WordPress installation.

  • Custom modular Gutenberg blocks allowing the marketing team to deploy new service pages quickly
  • Optimized core web vitals achieving sub-1.8s load times across global testing nodes
  • Secure RFP form handling with automated email notifications to sales and technical directors
  • Fully responsive design verified across mobile, tablet, and ultra-wide desktop monitors
