High-Intent Real Estate Lead Generation & HNI Acquisition Funnels

Precision Meta Ads targeting, luxury property showcases, and structured pre-qualification funnels for a high-growth real estate consultancy in Gurgaon.

Client
4S Realty Ventures
Industry
Real Estate
Channels
Meta Ads
Duration
4 months
Objective
Generate verified inquiries for premium residential & commercial projects
Location
India
01 · The challenge

High real estate ad spend wasted on invalid numbers and unqualified inquiries

Selling premium and luxury real estate in competitive markets like Gurgaon often results in ad campaigns swamped by brokers, job seekers, and unverified contacts.

  • Previous lead campaigns suffered from low connection rates due to autofilled inaccurate contact numbers.
  • Inquiries lacked budget alignment, inquiring about multi-crore properties without financial capability.
  • Ad creative felt like generic architectural renders without highlighting location advantages or developer credentials.
02 · Campaign strategy

HNI targeting, verified qualification, and bespoke site visit funnels

We structured ad sets around high-value affinity audiences, combining high-resolution project walkthroughs with explicit budget qualification steps in the lead form.

Audience
Affluent professionals, corporate executives, and investors aged 32–60 interested in luxury properties in Delhi NCR
Offer
Exclusive pre-launch pricing, private site walkthroughs, and guaranteed flexible payment plans
Creative
Architectural video tours, floor plan blueprints, and infrastructure connectivity maps (Dwarka Expressway, Golf Course Extension)
Meta Ads (Facebook & Instagram Feed, Stories, Reels)
Step 01 Project Showcase

Video walkthrough highlighting luxury amenities, clubhouse features, and strategic location connectivity.

Step 02 Higher Intent Lead Form

Lead Form with manual phone number entry and mandatory investment budget selection.

Step 03 WhatsApp Brochure Delivery

Instant delivery of detailed floor plans and pricing sheets via WhatsApp.

Step 04 Consultant Discovery

Senior relationship manager schedules private on-site inspection tour.

03 · Execution

What actually went live.

01

Precision demographic and interest layering

Layered frequent international travelers, luxury vehicle owners, and business leadership titles with real estate investment behaviors.

02

Higher Intent Form configuration

Swapped standard instant forms for Meta’s "Higher Intent" format with review steps, preventing accidental one-tap form submissions.

03

Budget band qualifying questions

Required prospects to select their investment horizon and budget range (e.g., ₹1.5 Cr–₹3 Cr, ₹3 Cr–₹5 Cr+).

04

Dynamic video creative rotation

Tested drone site footage, sample apartment walk-throughs, and location infrastructure spotlights.

04 · Optimisation

Decision, reason, outcome.

Decision

Swapped autofill phone fields for manual verification

Reason

Autofilled numbers often contained obsolete secondary SIM numbers.

Outcome

Lead connection rate on first outbound calls jumped significantly.

Decision

Added explicit starting price into ad copy and hero creatives

Reason

Hiding prices attracted casual aspirational lookers who could not afford luxury segments.

Outcome

Reduced unqualified volume while increasing high-ticket serious buyer conversations.

Decision

Segmented campaigns by project category (Commercial vs. Residential)

Reason

Commercial investors seek rental yields while residential buyers evaluate lifestyle and schools.

Outcome

Allowed tailored creative and distinct sales team allocation.

06 · Business result

High-value pipeline of verified property buyers

The campaign established a consistent stream of qualified investor leads, enabling sales consultants to conduct focused site walkthroughs.

Dramatically cut wasted sales rep call time by eliminating invalid numbers and budget mismatches.

Created a steady calendar of confirmed private site visits at flagship residential developments.

Key takeaway

In high-ticket luxury real estate, friction is a feature, not a bug. Introducing transparent pricing and manual form verification weeds out casual scrollers and leaves you with genuine buyers.
