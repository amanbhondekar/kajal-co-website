Our Works / Performance Marketing / Blythe Living

UK E-Commerce Paid Acquisition & Creative Funnel Restructuring

Transforming paid social performance for a premium UK outdoor furniture retailer through segmented Meta Ads campaigns, dynamic catalog feeds, and high-converting landing experiences.

Industry
Outdoor Furniture & Home Living
Service
Paid Social (Meta Ads) & E-Commerce Analytics
Market
United Kingdom
Duration
12 months
01 · The challenge

High-ticket seasonal furniture struggling with ad fatigue and broad targeting

Blythe Living offers premium garden sets, pergolas, and outdoor loungers. Outdoor furniture in the UK is heavily seasonal and carries a high average order value, making generic ad strategies inefficient.

  • Previous campaigns relied on single broad audiences without segmenting first-time buyers from warm catalog visitors.
  • Ad creative suffered from rapid fatigue, running single product images without showing lifestyle garden context or material durability.
  • High cart abandonment on mobile due to lack of trust badging and financing visibility.
02 · Campaign strategy

Full-funnel segmentation and seasonal creative sprints

We rebuilt the advertising account from the ground up, isolating cold prospecting from mid-funnel consideration and dynamic product retargeting.

Audience
UK homeowners aged 30–65 interested in garden design, home renovation, and premium outdoor living
Offer
Curated seasonal garden sets with free UK mainland delivery and 5-year weather-resistant warranty
Creative
UGC lifestyle styling clips, carousel feature showcases, and weather-durability demonstrations
Meta Ads (Facebook & Instagram)Shopify Dynamic CatalogGoogle Analytics GA4
Step 01 Top of Funnel (Cold Prospecting)

Broad lifestyle video ads showcasing premium garden transformations and weatherproofing.

Step 02 Middle of Funnel (Engagement & Traffic)

Carousel ads highlighting specific outdoor collections with customer reviews and dimensions.

Step 03 Bottom of Funnel (Dynamic Retargeting)

DPA catalog ads displaying viewed products paired with delivery guarantees and warranty trust.

Step 04 Post-Click Landing Experience

Collection-matched Shopify landing pages featuring finance options and fast checkout.

03 · Execution

What actually went live.

01

Disciplined account consolidation

Consolidated fragmented ad sets into structured testing and scaling campaigns to feed Meta’s algorithm maximum conversion data.

02

Weekly creative sprint framework

Implemented a continuous testing schedule rotating static lifestyle imagery, customer unboxing videos, and dimension overlays.

03

Dynamic product ads (DPA) feed optimization

Cleaned the Shopify product feed to include enriched collection tags, custom image overlays, and live inventory status.

04

Landing page conversion alignment

Aligned ad messaging directly with product detail headlines, highlighting UK mainland delivery and weather protection.

04 · Optimisation

Decision, reason, outcome.

Decision

Separated garden dining sets from lounge furniture in prospecting

Reason

Dining and lounging buyers have distinct room dimensions and price tolerances.

Outcome

Higher engagement relevance and reduced wasted impressions on mismatched collections.

Decision

Introduced UGC and customer patio transformation videos

Reason

Studio product packshots failed to answer how furniture looks in authentic British gardens.

Outcome

Click-through rates improved across cold interest segments.

Decision

Added Klarna and payment installment badges to retargeting creative

Reason

High purchase price caused hesitation at final checkout steps.

Outcome

Lower cart drop-off and faster buying decisions on dynamic retargeting ads.

06 · Business result

A sustainable acquisition engine for UK home living

The restructured Meta Ads funnel established a predictable acquisition pipeline that capitalized on seasonal demand spikes while maintaining efficiency.

Eliminated ad set cannibalization by establishing clean audience exclusions between cold, warm, and hot segments.

Established an agile creative pipeline that refreshed assets before frequency fatigue set in.

Key takeaway

In high-ticket home and garden retail, ad creative must sell the lifestyle transformation before the product specs. Pairing authentic lifestyle creative with disciplined catalog retargeting created a robust acquisition system.
