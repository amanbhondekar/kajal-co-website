Our Works / SEO / Jungle World

Local SEO & Organic Search Architecture for Family Entertainment in Blackpool

Dominating high-intent local family searches, optimizing Google Business Profiles, and building category-defining party booking pages for a premier Blackpool indoor play centre.

Industry
Kids Entertainment & Indoor Play
Service
Technical SEO, Local SEO, Content Architecture
Market
Blackpool & Lancashire, UK
Duration
12 months
01 · The challenge

Invisibility in local search for high-value family and party bookings

Jungle World offers expansive indoor soft play, climbing structures, and dedicated party rooms in Blackpool. Despite strong on-premise reviews, parents searching online for weekend activities and party venues were landing on competing venues.

  • Website was a single static page with no dedicated URLs for birthday parties, toddler sessions, or cafe menus.
  • Google Business Profile was unoptimized, missing key local categories, photo updates, and structured attribute tags.
  • Local citations were inconsistent across regional Lancashire directories, confusing search engines.
02 · SEO strategy

Location authority, service landing pages, and party booking hubs

We re-architected the site into a structured local entity, building dedicated landing pages for every attraction and optimizing local citation signals across Lancashire.

01

Entity & Local Map Optimization

Fully overhauled Google Business Profile with verified categories (Indoor Play Park, Children’s Party Service), weekly photo updates, and review generation.

02

Party Booking Architecture

Created dedicated landing pages for toddler parties, private venue hire, and themed birthday celebrations with pricing and FAQs.

03

Local Content Clusters

Published hyper-local guides for parents ("Things to do with toddlers in Blackpool on rainy days", "Best kids party venues in Lancashire").

04

Local Citation Consistency

Standardized NAP (Name, Address, Phone) data across 40+ UK business and tourism directories.

03 · What we changed

The work, in four buckets.

01

Technical & On-Page SEO

Implemented structured schema markup (LocalBusiness, Event, FAQPage) and fixed mobile usability issues across the WordPress site.

02

Party Venue Landing Pages

Built conversion-focused service pages detailing package inclusions, dietary options, and online party booking inquiries.

03

Google Business Profile Management

Systematized GBP posting, added interactive Q&As, and tagged seasonal holiday opening hours.

04

Local Authority Citation Cleanup

Audited and corrected conflicting legacy telephone numbers and address variations across UK business listings.

05 · Business impact

The go-to destination for family weekends and celebrations

Organic search became the primary engine driving birthday party inquiries and weekend admissions.

Transformed the website from a forgotten digital brochure into an active inquiry driver for high-margin party bookings.

Established dominant organic presence for parents searching for indoor activities during school holidays and rainy weekends.
