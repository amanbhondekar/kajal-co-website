Our Works / Website Design / Aarshaveda

Modernising Heritage Ayurveda for Digital D2C Commerce

Responsive Shopify architecture, authentic botanical storytelling, and conversion-focused product pages bringing a 1945 legacy Ayurvedic brand into modern digital retail.

Industry
Ayurveda & Wellness
Service
UX/UI Design & Shopify Development
Location
Kerala, India
Duration
7 months
01 · The challenge

A legacy healing tradition lost in modern e-commerce complexity

Aarshaveda carries generational formulation expertise dating to 1945, but modern digital shoppers found traditional Ayurvedic catalog presentations intimidating and fragmented.

Dense Sanskrit terminology and clinical descriptions overwhelmed buyers looking for accessible daily botanical wellness.

Customers required immediate clarity on pure ingredients, safety certifications, and therapeutic benefits before purchasing premium baby care and wellness formulations.

Mobile visitors accounted for majority of discovery traffic but faced navigation friction on legacy layouts.

Redesign and engineer a high-converting Shopify storefront that preserves heritage authenticity while delivering effortless modern shopping.

02 · The strategy

Heritage meets purity: an editorial wellness experience

We developed an organic visual design system and restructured product storytelling around personal wellness routines rather than botanical textbooks.

01Earthy organic palette paired with clean editorial typography
02Benefit-first product detail hierarchy with visible ingredient provenance
03Adaptive responsive viewports with sticky mobile checkout triggers
04Transparent quality standards and AYUSH certifications highlighted at decision points
03 · The experience

The decisions that did the work.

Decision 01

Ingredient transparency and benefit-led hero blocks

Every product page opens with primary therapeutic benefits, followed by illustrated ingredient breakdowns and clinical certifications before pricing.

Why it mattered

Wellness buyers make decisions based on ingredient safety and purity. Leading with botanical provenance builds immediate buying confidence.

Decision 02

Curated wellness routines replacing isolated SKUs

Grouped complementary treatments into morning rituals, baby care routines, and restorative regimens with single-click bundle additions.

Why it mattered

Encourages regimen-based healing while increasing average order value naturally without aggressive sales banners.

Decision 03

Zero-friction mobile checkout architecture

Integrated persistent sticky cart actions, instant pincode delivery checks, and multi-gateway express payments.

Why it mattered

Removes checkout hesitation on mobile devices where over 70% of initial wellness discovery occurs.

04 · The design system

Botanical design language built for scale

A bespoke visual identity balancing traditional Ayurvedic warmth with modern digital accessibility.

Typography
Warm serif display headings paired with high-legibility geometric sans-serif body
Colour Palette
Earthy botanical sage, warm terracotta accents, and soft cream surfaces
Components
Modular Shopify sections for ingredients, customer verification, and routines
Responsiveness
Fluid layouts optimized for mobile, tablet, and desktop viewports
05 · Implementation

Lightweight Shopify theme engineering

Engineered clean liquid templates stripped of unneeded third-party scripts to guarantee sub-2s mobile loading.

  • Custom modular Liquid sections manageable directly by the in-house merchandising team
  • Fast responsive image delivery pipeline optimized for 4G mobile connections
  • Full catalog categorization by health goal (Baby Care, Immunity, Daily Vitality)
  • Native Shopify review integration and trust badge certification framework
