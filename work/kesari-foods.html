Our Works / SEO / Kesari Foods
Food &amp; Beverage · SEO

Non-brand search traffic up 5.8×

Clearing a slow, duplicated catalogue for a speciality food brand, then building the category pages the range deserved.

Client
Kesari Foods
Industry
Speciality food · D2C
Service
Technical SEO, content
Market
India
Duration
6 months
01 · The challenge

Nine thousand pages, four hundred products

Every filter combination on the catalogue generated a crawlable URL. Google was spending its crawl budget on near-duplicate pages and ranking none of them.

  • 9,100 indexed URLs against roughly 400 real products
  • Category pages carried no copy, so they competed with each other on identical titles
  • Median mobile load of 5.2s on the pages that mattered most
  • Non-brand organic was under 8% of sessions
02 · SEO strategy

Remove, then build

Two phases, deliberately sequential: cut the catalogue down to the pages worth ranking, and only then write the content that would rank them.

01

Crawl control

Faceted URLs moved behind canonical tags and robots rules, taking the index from 9,100 pages to 640 without losing a single real product.

02

Category as landing page

Each category became a page about the ingredient — what it is, how it is graded, how to use it — with the products below.

03

Recipe-led support content

Recipes were built where search demand already existed, each linking into the category it uses rather than sitting in an isolated blog.

04

Speed

Image pipeline, deferred scripts and a lighter template brought median mobile load from 5.2s to 1.7s.

03 · What we changed

The work, in four buckets.

01

Technical SEO

Canonicalised faceted navigation, cleared 8,400 near-duplicate URLs from the index, and fixed the pagination that was orphaning deep catalogue pages.

02

On-page SEO

Wrote unique titles, headings and 300–500 words of genuinely useful copy on 38 category pages.

03

Content

Published 46 recipes chosen on existing search demand, each linking to the categories it draws from.

04

Site structure

Connected recipes, categories and products into clusters, so informational traffic has somewhere commercial to go.

05 · Business impact

Organic became the cheapest shelf

Clearing the index did not add traffic by itself. It made the content that followed effective, which is where the growth came from.

Recipe traffic now feeds category pages that sell, so informational content earns its place rather than sitting in a blog nobody links to.

Organic revenue overtook paid social in month five, which changed how the brand plans its acquisition mix rather than just its reporting.
