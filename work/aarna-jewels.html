Our Works / Website Design / Aarna Jewels
Jewellery · Website Design

Enquiries up 3.2× in 90 days

Rebuilding a jewellery brand’s site around the enquiry rather than the catalogue, so showroom-quality conversations start online.

Client
Aarna Jewels
Industry
Fine jewellery · D2C
Services
UX, visual design, build
Platform
Shopify
Timeline
6 weeks
01 · The challenge

A catalogue that stopped short of the conversation

Aarna sells pieces that people want to see, hold and ask about before buying. The old site behaved like a lookbook: it showed the range beautifully and then left visitors with a single contact form buried in the footer.

Product pages ended at a price and a photograph. There was no way to ask about resizing, stone substitution or a custom commission — the three questions that start almost every sale.

Mobile traffic was the majority of sessions and the minority of enquiries. The form was the narrowest part of the funnel and it was also the hardest thing to fill in on a phone.

Turn browsing into a booked consultation without turning the brand into a discount store.

02 · The strategy

Design the enquiry, then design around it

We treated the enquiry as the product. Every template was then judged on one question: does this page make the next conversation easier to start?

Put a contextual enquiry on every product page
Lead with craft and provenance, not price
Collapse the journey to three steps on mobile
Make custom commissions a visible path, not a footnote
03 · The experience

The decisions that did the work.

Decision 01

An enquiry form that already knows the piece

Each product page carries its own enquiry panel, pre-filled with the piece, the metal and the size being viewed. The visitor adds a question and a phone number; nothing else is asked.

Why it mattered

The old form asked people to describe what they were looking at. Pre-filling removed the step most likely to lose a mobile visitor mid-thought, and gave the sales team a qualified brief instead of a name and an email address.

Decision 02

Custom commissions given their own journey

Bespoke work moved out of a paragraph on the About page into a dedicated path with its own gallery, timeline and starting price band.

Why it mattered

Commissions are the highest-value work and the hardest to explain. Showing the process and the honest timeline up front attracted better-matched enquiries and filtered out the ones that were never going to convert.

Decision 03

Craft shown before the price

Product templates open with material, origin and making detail. Price sits below the fold, next to the enquiry, rather than competing with the first photograph.

Why it mattered

Buyers at this price point decide on trust first. Leading with provenance gave the price somewhere to land.

04 · The design system

A small system the team can actually run

Enough structure to keep 200 product pages consistent, and no more than the in-house team could maintain without a designer on call.

Typography
Two weights, one display face, a fixed six-step scale
Colour
Warm neutral base, single accent reserved for enquiry actions
Components
14 reusable sections, each with a mobile rule
Imagery
Fixed crop ratios so new product shots drop in without reflowing
05 · Implementation

Built as sections, not as pages

The approved design shipped as a responsive Shopify theme assembled from reusable sections, so the team can build a new landing page without needing a developer or a redesign.

  • 14 section types, each configurable from the Shopify editor
  • Enquiry submissions routed to the sales inbox and to the CRM
  • Product imagery served responsively; largest contentful paint under 2s on 4G
  • Collection and campaign pages composable from the same sections
