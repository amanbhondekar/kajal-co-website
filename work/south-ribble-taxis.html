Transportation & Taxi Services · SEO

High-Intent Local Dispatch SEO & Station Transfer Visibility in South Ribble

Capturing immediate local taxi bookings, airport transfer inquiries, and corporate transport searches across South Ribble and Central Lancashire.

Client
South Ribble Taxis
Industry
Transportation & Taxi Services
Service
Local SEO, Click-to-Call Optimization, Technical Architecture
Market
South Ribble, Leyland & Preston, UK
Duration
8 months
01 · The challenge

Losing on-demand and airport transfer passengers to national ride apps

South Ribble Taxis provides 24/7 private hire, wheelchair accessible transport, and scheduled airport runs. Passengers searching on smartphones needed immediate telephone dispatch and upfront pricing, but the firm was buried behind generic aggregators.

  • Mobile visitors had to search through multiple pages just to find a telephone number to call.
  • No landing pages existed for lucrative airport transfer routes (Manchester Airport, Liverpool John Lennon Airport).
  • Google Business Profile lacked coverage for key regional service areas including Leyland, Penwortham, and Bamber Bridge.
02 · SEO strategy

Mobile click-to-call prominence and regional destination hubs

We rebuilt the website around mobile urgency, featuring one-tap dispatch calling, transparent airport fare estimates, and sub-region landing pages.

01

Mobile Click-to-Call Architecture

Designed persistent top and bottom telephone dispatch buttons, enabling travelers to initiate calls within one tap.

02

Airport Transfer Destination Hubs

Built dedicated landing pages for Manchester Airport and Liverpool Airport transfers with flight monitoring and flat-rate pricing.

03

Sub-Region Local Coverage

Created targeted pages for key commuter hubs: Leyland Station, Penwortham, Bamber Bridge, and Lostock Hall.

04

TaxiService Schema Markup

Implemented structured data defining fleet vehicle types, service areas, and 24/7 operating availability.

03 · What we changed

The work, in four buckets.

01

Mobile Usability & Speed Overhaul

Optimized page load times down to sub-1.5s on mobile networks to support travelers needing immediate transport.

02

Local Landing Page Creation

Published targeted location pages answering specific queries ("taxi near Leyland train station", "Preston to Manchester Airport taxi").

03

Google Business Profile Enhancements

Expanded defined service areas, updated 24-hour dispatch hours, and monitored customer feedback.

04

Fare Calculator & Inquiry Form

Implemented a lightweight quotation form for advance corporate bookings and long-distance transfers.

05 · Business impact

Dominant dispatch visibility when passengers need a ride most

Mobile local search established South Ribble Taxis as the primary private hire choice across the borough.

Secured consistent incoming telephone calls from passengers at transit hubs and local commercial centers.

Built a profitable pipeline of scheduled airport transfers booked days in advance.
