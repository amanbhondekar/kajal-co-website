Hospitality · SEO

Direct bookings up 2.9×, commission down

Winning back direct bookings for a boutique hotel group that had handed its search presence to the aggregators.

Client
Serai Hotels
Industry
Boutique hospitality
Service
Technical SEO, content, local SEO
Market
Goa · Jaipur · Coorg
Duration
11 months
01 · The challenge

Outranked on its own name

Aggregators occupied the first three results for every Serai property, including searches for the hotel by name. Each of those bookings cost the group 15–22% in commission.

  • Property pages were thin and nearly identical across the three locations
  • No content about the surrounding area, which is most of what guests research
  • Booking engine on a separate subdomain with no internal linking
  • Reviews scattered across platforms, none structured for search
02 · SEO strategy

Be the best page about your own hotel

Aggregators have authority but generic content. The one thing they cannot do is know the property and its neighbourhood better than the people who run it.

01

Property depth

Every room type, rate, policy and facility documented properly — more detail than any aggregator listing carries.

02

Neighbourhood guides

What to do within walking distance, written by the staff who actually live there.

03

Booking continuity

Moved the booking engine onto the main domain so the path from search to booking stays on one site.

04

Structured reviews

Consolidated and marked up reviews so ratings surface in the result itself.

03 · What we changed

The work, in four buckets.

01

Technical SEO

Migrated the booking engine onto the main domain, consolidating authority that had been split across two hostnames for four years.

02

On-page SEO

Rebuilt three property pages and nineteen room-type pages, each with full rates, policies and facilities.

03

Content

Published 27 neighbourhood guides written by property staff, each linking to the property it surrounds.

04

Local SEO

Consolidated review signals and added hotel structured data so ratings and prices appear in the result.

05 · Business impact

The same room night, a larger share of it

Shifting bookings from aggregator to direct does not add guests. It changes what each guest is worth.

Direct share moved from 21% to 49% of bookings, and every point of that shift is commission the group keeps.

Direct guests also book longer stays on average, which the group attributes to the neighbourhood guides setting up a longer itinerary before arrival.
