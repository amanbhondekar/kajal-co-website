Our Works / Website Design / MKPS & Associates LLP

Authoritative Digital Presence for Premier Chartered Accountants

A modern, high-credibility corporate website reflecting institutional rigour, tax advisory expertise, and regulatory compliance for an established Mumbai CA firm.

Industry
Accounting & Finance
Service
Website Development & Wix Architecture
Location
Mumbai, India
Duration
7 months
01 · The challenge

Decades of institutional trust masked by an outdated web presence

MKPS & Associates LLP serves multinational corporations, growing enterprises, and HNIs in statutory audit, corporate taxation, and financial compliance. However, their digital footprint failed to match their industry standing.

Complex multidisciplinary services (Audit, Direct Tax, GST, Corporate Law, Valuation) were tangled into single dense text pages.

Prospective corporate clients could not quickly locate partner credentials, firm affiliations, or industry experience.

Lacked responsive design, creating a poor first impression when accessed by corporate decision-makers on mobile devices.

Design and deploy a distinguished, responsive corporate web presence in Figma and Wix that establishes institutional authority and facilitates corporate inquiries.

02 · The strategy

Clarity, regulatory gravity, and partner authority

We structured the firm’s digital presence around practice area expertise, sector specializations, and clear corporate engagement pathways.

01Classic corporate navy and crisp white palette conveying stability and financial trust
02Structured practice area hubs with regulatory scope and advisory solutions
03Partner profile directory highlighting professional credentials, ICAI affiliations, and experience
04Secure corporate consultation inquiry channel with preliminary engagement brief
03 · The experience

The decisions that did the work.

Decision 01

Categorized practice area architecture

Reorganized 15+ specialized services into 4 core pillars: Audit & Assurance, Direct & Indirect Taxation, Corporate Advisory, and Regulatory Compliance.

Why it mattered

Corporate CFOs and directors find relevant expertise within seconds without wading through general accounting copy.

Decision 02

Partner credentials and firm governance showcase

Developed dedicated partner profiles detailing leadership experience, certifications, and sector track records.

Why it mattered

In professional services, clients hire trusted advisors. Leading with partner profiles establishes human credibility.

Decision 03

Corporate engagement inquiry brief

Replaced vague generic forms with a structured corporate intake requesting organization type, service requirement, and timeline.

Why it mattered

Filters spam and delivers actionable context to the firm’s engagement partners immediately.

Financial institutional system

A commanding corporate design system reflecting stability, precision, and confidentiality.

05 · Implementation

Figma to Wix corporate engineering

Iterated through comprehensive Figma design prototypes before production build on Wix Studio with full responsive precision.

  • Figma UI library with complete breakpoint wireframes for desktop, laptop, tablet, and mobile
  • Clean, accessible Wix Studio implementation adhering to strict regulatory professional norms
  • Automated corporate contact routing into designated practice lead inboxes
  • Fast page load times with crisp vector typography and optimized corporate assets
