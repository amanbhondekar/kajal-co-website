## Hero

### Paid Acquisition Structured for High-Consideration Home Living

How structured Meta campaigns, catalogue integrations, and attribution discipline supported a 12-month direct-to-consumer acquisition channel across the UK.

## Project Overview

Blythe Living is a UK-based outdoor furniture and home living brand offering premium dining sets, garden furniture, and artisan living pieces. Because home furnishings represent considered purchases with multi-touch buyer journeys, customer acquisition required a deliberate paid strategy that balanced broad visual inspiration with sharp commercial intent. Over a 12-month engagement, Kajal & Co. led performance marketing campaigns across Meta Ads, working in close alignment with the brand's Shopify store and Google Analytics infrastructure.

## The Challenge

Selling high-value home and outdoor living items online involves distinct acquisition hurdles:

- **Long Consideration Cycles:** Customers rarely purchase high-ticket dining sets or outdoor lounge collections on first impression, requiring continuous nurture rather than impulse-buy messaging.
- **Seasonal Demand Fluctuations:** Garden and outdoor living demand surges in spring and summer, requiring rapid budget scaling, followed by a seasonal transition to indoor collections in autumn and winter.
- **Attribution & Feed Synchronisation:** Inaccurate catalog data or weak event tracking leads to wasted spend on out-of-stock items or misattributed multi-channel purchases.

## Objective

- Build a sustainable, full-funnel Meta Ads engine that introduces qualified UK homeowners to the brand.
- Create automated dynamic retargeting flows connected directly to the Shopify product catalogue.
- Establish clean conversion tracking in Google Analytics to measure true purchase intent across customer journeys.

## Strategy

The campaign strategy was built on three core pillars:

1. **Visual Discovery & Problem-Solving Creative:** Meta prospecting sets tested lifestyle imagery, living space inspirations, and specific product collections (oak dining collections, weatherproof outdoor furniture) against broad and interest-refined UK homeowner audiences.
2. **Product Catalogue Feeds & Dynamic Retargeting:** Shopify product feeds were structured to serve personalized carousel and collection ads to shoppers who browsed specific categories or added items to cart without purchasing.
3. **Seasonal Pacing & Stock-Aware Budgeting:** Budgets were dynamically allocated toward in-stock high-margin hero products while phasing promotions to mirror natural seasonal demand spikes.

## Execution

- **Campaign Hierarchy:** Structured campaigns into distinct top-of-funnel discovery, mid-funnel consideration (engagement with collections), and bottom-of-funnel catalogue sales.
- **Shopify & Meta Pixel Alignment:** Ensured server-side event tracking, ViewContent, AddToCart, and Purchase events fired accurately, minimising signal loss.
- **Creative Refresh Cycles:** Continuously rotated ad creative to prevent audience fatigue, spotlighting craftsmanship details, dimensions, and customer delivery guarantees.
- **Cross-Channel Measurement:** Monitored blended acquisition efficiency alongside Google Analytics to ensure paid social complemented organic search and direct traffic without cannibalisation.

## Results

Over the 12-month engagement, Meta Ads became an established paid acquisition engine for Blythe Living across the United Kingdom. The brand maintained active paid prospecting and retargeting throughout peak seasonal cycles, feeding qualified buyers into the Shopify store and establishing predictable customer acquisition for high-ticket home goods.

## Key Takeaways

- Considered home goods require multi-stage paid funnels where inspiration leads and catalogue retargeting closes.
- Direct alignment between Shopify inventory feeds and Meta Ads prevents wasted ad spend on sold-out collections.
- Treating paid acquisition as a complementary channel to organic search creates stronger overall brand stability.
