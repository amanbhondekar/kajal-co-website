## Hero

### Compounding Organic Search Traffic for Premium British Home Living

How high-intent product collection architecture, technical Shopify optimisation, and commercial keyword targeting built an organic acquisition channel driving high-value furniture sales across the UK.

## Project Overview

Blythe Living is a British furniture and living brand specializing in oak dining collections, handcrafted garden furniture, and artisan home accessories. For a direct-to-consumer lifestyle brand, organic search provides an invaluable foundation of intent-driven, high-margin customer acquisition that offsets rising paid media costs. Over a 12-month engagement, Kajal & Co. led the SEO strategy for Blythe Living, structuring the Shopify store to rank for competitive non-brand commercial searches and converting organic discovery into verified e-commerce revenue.

## The Challenge

E-commerce search in the home and living category presents complex technical and content hurdles:

- **Heavy Organic Competition:** Ranking against established UK department stores and big-box furniture retailers requires laser focus on specific material, style, and room-intent queries.
- **Shopify Collection Architecture:** Default Shopify structures often suffer from duplicate collection pagination, weak internal linking, and thin collection descriptions that fail to satisfy search intent.
- **High-Ticket Cart Consideration:** Capturing search users who are ready to purchase £500+ dining tables and oak chests requires detailed product schema, transparent dimensions, and clear delivery signals right on the search landing page.

## Objective

- Build a sustainable organic search channel that ranks for high-intent furniture and seasonal living searches across the UK.
- Optimize Shopify category architecture, metadata, and product schema to improve crawl efficiency and organic click-through rates.
- Connect organic search performance directly to verified Shopify transactions and revenue tracked in Google Analytics.

## Strategy

1. **Material & Dimension Keyword Targeting:** Targeted long-tail commercial queries matching exact customer intent (e.g., solid oak dining chairs, slatted round dining tables, portable stainless outdoor burners, Nordman fir trees) rather than broad, vanity keywords.
2. **Shopify Category & Internal Linking Restructure:** Enriched core collection pages with comprehensive buyer guidance, care instructions, and logical faceted internal links connecting dining chairs to complementary dining tables.
3. **Structured Data & Product Schema:** Implemented full Product, Offer, AggregateRating, and Breadcrumb schema markup to win rich search snippets showcasing price, in-stock availability, and product specifications.

## Execution

- **Collection Page Optimization:** Rewrote title tags, meta descriptions, and on-page headings across high-margin collections, aligning copy with UK search volume patterns.
- **Seasonal Landing Page Strategy:** Built dedicated search assets for recurring seasonal demand spikes (such as outdoor garden entertaining in early summer and festive Nordman fir collections in late autumn).
- **Technical Audit & Cleanup:** Resolved canonical tag conflicts, optimized image payloads for mobile performance, and removed orphan URL parameters generated by filtering apps.
- **E-commerce Attribution Setup:** Connected GA4 e-commerce tracking to monitor the full organic conversion funnel from initial search landing through add-to-cart, checkout initiation, and final purchase.

## Results

Over the 12-month engagement, organic search matured into a dependable source of customer acquisition for Blythe Living. Verified Google Analytics data demonstrated strong engagement across British homeowners, with organic search driving hundreds of qualified shopping sessions and dozens of direct purchases of high-value furniture pieces.

## Key Takeaways

- In luxury home furnishings, organic search delivers an educated buyer with significantly higher average engagement time (58s) and higher purchase values.
- Targeting precise product specifications (e.g., California Oak, Soho Slatted, Burford 3-Drawer) captures buyers at the exact moment of commercial readiness.
- Seasonal evergreen landing pages (like Christmas trees and outdoor summer living) compound in authority when retained and updated annually.
