## Project Overview

Little Mowgli Nursery is a day nursery and early years childcare provider located in Leyland, Lancashire. In early childhood education, choosing a nursery is an emotionally intense, high-trust decision governed by proximity, safety reputation, and funded childcare availability. Over a 10-month SEO partnership, Kajal & Co. developed and executed a targeted local search engine optimization strategy to position Little Mowgli Nursery as the premier early years choice for parents across Leyland and surrounding South Ribble communities.

## The Challenge

Marketing early years childcare centres involves strict trust and geographical parameters:

- **Hyper-Local Catchment Constraints:** Parents typically only consider nurseries within a 10-to-15 minute commute of their home or workplace, making broad regional rankings irrelevant.
- **High Trust Barrier:** Unlike standard commercial services, parents require deep reassurance regarding staff qualifications, Early Years Foundation Stage (EYFS) curriculum, safety standards, and Ofsted ratings before making contact.
- **Specific Funding Queries:** Parents actively search for specific government-funded hours (e.g., 15 & 30 hours funded childcare, 2-year-old funding, tax-free childcare schemes), requiring clear, authoritative explanatory content.

## Objective

- Achieve top-3 local map pack and organic visibility for high-intent nursery searches in Leyland, Farington, and surrounding areas.
- Create clear informational landing pages explaining nursery rooms (baby room, toddler room, preschool) and government funding eligibility.
- Streamline the conversion path on mobile devices to drive direct nursery tour bookings and registration enquiries.

## Strategy

1. **Local Search Intent & Proximity Targeting:** Focused on high-intent local queries such as "nursery in Leyland", "day nursery near me", "preschool Leyland", and "funded childcare Leyland".
2. **Age-Stage Room & Program Architecture:** Built dedicated sub-pages for distinct age groups—Babies (0–2), Toddlers (2–3), and Preschool (3–5)—addressing specific parental developmental concerns for each stage.
3. **Local Map Pack & Reputation Engineering:** Optimized the nursery's Google Business Profile with rich photographic tours, updated session schedules, verified parent reviews, and structured local business schema markup.

## Execution

- **On-Page & Metadata Refinement:** Aligned page titles, meta descriptions, and header tags with regional geographic modifiers across Leyland and Preston borders.
- **Funded Childcare Hub:** Created a comprehensive parent guide detailing how 15 and 30 hours of government-funded childcare work at the nursery, answering common questions and winning featured local snippets.
- **Tour Booking Conversion Flow:** Simplified the website's booking inquiry form, adding a 1-click "Book a Nursery Tour" CTA prominently visible on mobile screens.
- **Analytics & Event Monitoring:** Configured Google Analytics event tracking to monitor key parent engagement signals: tour inquiry submissions, phone call clicks, and location map clicks.

## Results

Across the 10-month engagement, Little Mowgli Nursery firmly established local search dominance in Leyland. The nursery gained consistent top-ranking positions on Google Maps and local organic search, generating a predictable stream of visit requests and registration enquiries from local families seeking high-quality early years care.

## Key Takeaways

- For nurseries and schools, local map presence is the single most valuable organic real estate; parents evaluate distance first.
- Transparently answering complex government funding questions positions a nursery as an approachable, expert partner in early parenting.
- Reducing friction on tour booking forms directly increases nursery walk-in conversion rates.
