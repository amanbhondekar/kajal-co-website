## Hero

### High-Intent Lead Generation for Premium Real Estate

How structured Meta ad funnels, project positioning, and upfront budget filtering delivered high-intent property buyer enquiries.

## Project Overview

4S Realty Ventures is a real estate advisory and development firm marketing residential and commercial developments in India. In real estate advertising, lead volume is notoriously easy to inflate, but unqualified leads burn sales team bandwidth and drive up effective acquisition costs. Over a 4-month campaign window, Kajal & Co. structured and managed targeted Meta Ads campaigns designed to attract serious property buyers, filter out unqualified submissions, and drive booked site visits.

## The Challenge

Property advertising on social media frequently suffers from systemic qualification issues:

- **Inflated Lead Numbers, Low Intent:** Auto-fill lead forms without friction produce submissions from users with no immediate purchasing power or timeline.
- **Budget Mismatch:** Enquiries frequently fall outside the price bracket of the developments being represented.
- **High Sales Team Fatigue:** When sales executives spend hours contacting unresponsive numbers, morale drops and legitimate leads cool down before receiving dedicated attention.

## Objective

- Generate a steady flow of verified buyer enquiries interested in specific project configurations and price bands.
- Introduce friction into Meta lead forms to filter for genuine buyer capacity and intended move-in or investment timelines.
- Position project visual assets, floor plans, and amenities to establish immediate architectural and location credibility.

## Strategy

1. **Qualification-First Lead Architecture:** Built custom multi-step Meta Instant Forms requiring active selections: preferred unit type (e.g., 2 BHK, 3 BHK, Penthouse, Retail space), investment horizon (ready-to-move vs. under-construction), and budget range confirmation.
2. **Project-Led Visual Storytelling:** Highlighted tangible project advantages—connectivity, builder pedigree, floor plan layouts, and payment milestones—rather than vague luxury promises.
3. **Audience Refinement:** Targeted high-net-worth brackets, corporate business districts, and real estate investment interest clusters while excluding generic or irrelevant demographics.

## Execution

- **Campaign Segmentation:** Divided campaigns by project asset class—luxury residential living vs. commercial retail investments—ensuring copy and creative matched the specific buyer psychology.
- **Micro-Copy on Forms:** Added explicit disclaimers on forms stating the entry price point, ensuring applicants understood project scale before hitting submit.
- **Creative Variation Testing:** Tested carousel tours of layout plans against static hero renderings of clubhouse amenities and location maps to see which creative drove higher site-visit conversion.
- **Weekly Lead Quality Reviews:** Conducted regular feedback loops with the client's sales desk to review lead response rates and refine audience exclusions based on ground reality.

## Results

Across the 4-month engagement, 4S Realty Ventures transitioned from dealing with generic social clicks to working with structured, qualified buyer enquiries. The sales team experienced higher connect rates and more productive conversations, leading to booked site visits from buyers with verified budgets and clear purchase timelines.

## Key Takeaways

- In high-ticket real estate, intentional form friction increases lead quality and saves hundreds of hours of wasted sales calls.
- Clear disclosure of pricing floors in ad creative deters unqualified clicks and builds immediate buyer confidence.
- Tight feedback loops between the performance media buyer and the sales floor are essential to calibrate targeting weekly.
