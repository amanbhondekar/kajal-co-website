## Project Overview

M.D. Cargo & Movers provides professional relocation, packing, household shifting, and corporate transport services across the National Capital Region (Delhi, Gurgaon, Noida, Faridabad, and Ghaziabad) and inter-city routes. In the regional logistics industry, broad advertising often results in high volumes of price-shopping callers rather than genuine booking enquiries. Over a 6-month engagement, Kajal & Co. restructured the company's Meta Ads strategy to generate verified, route-specific moving enquiries.

## The Challenge

Logistics marketing in competitive urban hubs presents distinct operational difficulties:

- **High Price Sensitivity & Unqualified Leads:** Generic ads attract users looking for low-cost courier tasks rather than full household or office relocation services.
- **Geographic Fragmentation:** Moving demand is hyper-local; targeting needs to account for specific residential clusters, gated communities, and corporate commercial corridors.
- **Urgency vs. Pre-Planning:** Customers moving home usually seek quotes 1 to 3 weeks ahead of their lease transition, requiring tight timing and rapid follow-up workflows.

## Objective

- Establish a reliable stream of qualified relocation enquiries for residential and commercial shifting.
- Implement structured lead forms on Meta that capture essential moving details before submission.
- Focus advertising budget on high-value relocation routes across Delhi NCR and major interstate corridors.

## Strategy

1. **Pre-Qualifying Lead Architecture:** Replaced open-ended forms with structured on-platform Meta instant forms asking critical qualifying questions: move origin, destination, move size (1BHK, 2BHK, 3BHK, Villa, Office), and target move date.
2. **Hyper-Local & Intent-Driven Audiences:** Targeted residential hubs, tech parks, and commercial centres within Delhi NCR, layering demographic signals indicative of upcoming moves (job changes, newly moved, home improvement interests).
3. **Trust & Transparency Ad Creatives:** Designed ad sets addressing the primary anxiety of relocation customers: goods safety, professional packing footage, verified vehicle fleets, and damage-free guarantees.

## Execution

- **Lead Form Optimisation:** Configured forms with phone number verification and mandatory date selections, immediately filtering casual price-checkers.
- **Geographic Route Segmentation:** Segmented campaigns into intra-city moves (e.g., Gurgaon to Noida, South Delhi to Gurgaon) and major inter-state corridors (Delhi to Bangalore/Mumbai/Pune).
- **Fast Lead Delivery Pipeline:** Integrated instant notification triggers so sales coordinators received enquiries within minutes of form submission, when customer intent was highest.
- **Creative Rotation:** Tested static carousel breakdowns of packing stages alongside clear pricing transparency markers to establish immediate credibility.

## Results

Over the 6-month campaign, M.D. Cargo & Movers established an organized paid acquisition channel on Meta. The shift from generic traffic to structured, route-qualified lead forms enabled the operations team to focus sales calls on serious relocation clients with clear timelines and verified volume requirements.

## Key Takeaways

- In service logistics, lead qualification is more valuable than raw volume; filtering early protects the sales team's closing capacity.
- Localised copy mentioning specific NCR sectors and transit hubs builds trust faster than generic national messaging.
- Rapid lead response times directly correlate with closing rates in time-sensitive service industries.
