## Project Overview

DeAge is an Indian health and wellness brand operating at the cutting-edge intersection of longevity, healthy aging, functional nutrition, and personalized wellness programs. Positioned far beyond conventional supplement e-commerce, DeAge combines science-backed nutritional formulations with clinical health assessments, biological biomarker interpretation, and expert physician-led longevity protocols. To bring this multi-tiered offering to market, Kajal & Co. executed a 3-month UX/UI design and Shopify development sprint, establishing a digital experience that bridges consumer e-commerce with specialized health programs.

## The Challenge

Translating advanced longevity science into an accessible consumer digital journey presents unique user experience obstacles:

- **Dual Business Model Complexity:** The website needed to serve two distinct customer journeys simultaneously: instant-purchase D2C functional nutrition products, and multi-week personalized longevity health programs requiring health assessments and booking flows.
- **Scientific Credibility vs. Accessibility:** Longevity and cellular health science involve complex clinical concepts (cellular renewal, metabolic efficiency, biomarker tracking); the interface needed to communicate rigorous medical backing without overwhelming the non-expert shopper.
- **High-Consideration Decision Flow:** Premium wellness programs require a high degree of trust before purchase, demanding transparent program breakdowns, physician credentials, and interactive assessment touchpoints.

## Objective

- Design a comprehensive, modular UX/UI design system in Figma encompassing both e-commerce product pages and program consultation workflows.
- Create an intuitive Information Architecture (IA) guiding users from longevity education into appropriate product or program tiers.
- Develop a customized, high-performance Shopify storefront featuring tailored Product Detail Pages (PDPs) and seamless checkout flows.

## Strategy

1. **Integrated Product & Program Architecture:** Structured the information hierarchy into two complementary tracks: "Formulations" (direct functional nutrition) and "Programs" (personalized longevity journeys combining assessments, formulations, and doctor follow-up).
2. **Clinical Authority UI System:** Developed a clean, medical-grade yet lifestyle-warm design system in Figma—combining crisp typography, scientific verification modules, and clinical iconography with approachable human imagery.
3. **Interactive Program Detail Pages (PDPs):** Re-engineered the standard Shopify PDP into a comprehensive program guide outlining: "What's Included", biomarker tests, consultation milestones, weekly timelines, and clinical doctor oversight.

## Execution

- **Figma Design System & Style Guide:** Built a full component library detailing color tokens, accessible contrast standards, typography scales, clinical badge icons, and modular assessment widgets.
- **Custom Shopify Theme Development:** Implemented custom Liquid templates on Shopify to support rich, accordion-driven program breakdowns and formulation ingredient transparency panels.
- **Interactive Booking & Program Onboarding:** Designed and prototyped a guided program consultation flow allowing prospective longevity clients to book introductory health evaluations.
- **Frictionless Mobile Checkout:** Tailored mobile navigation to provide sticky action bars, clear pricing structures, and streamlined checkout sequences.

## Results

Within the 3-month engagement, DeAge successfully launched a comprehensive digital platform that established its position as a pioneer in India's emerging longevity market. The new Shopify site gave consumers an intuitive, credible environment to discover both advanced functional formulations and personalized health protocols, creating an authoritative digital foundation for scalable growth.

## Key Takeaways

- In longevity and preventative healthcare, educating the user on the science behind the formulation is the single most important driver of purchase confidence.
- Hybrid business models (products + clinical programs) require separate, dedicated page architectures to prevent buyer confusion.
- Modular PDP design systems allow clinical brands to add new formulations and protocol tiers rapidly without redesigning the core site.
