## Hero

### Scaling D2C Skincare Purchases Through Routine-Driven Paid Acquisition

How ingredient-led creative testing, Shopify conversion funnel tracking, and automated retargeting built an 8-month customer acquisition engine for luxury organic skincare.

## Project Overview

KishKin is an organic skincare and personal care brand offering cold-pressed botanicals, restorative eye formulations, and silk beauty essentials. In the competitive direct-to-consumer (D2C) beauty market, customer acquisition costs can quickly escalate without clear creative differentiation and tight funnel optimisation. Over an 8-month engagement, Kajal & Co. led performance marketing across Meta Ads, integrating the brand's Shopify storefront and Google Analytics to drive qualified online purchases.

## The Challenge

D2C beauty brands face significant paid acquisition pressure:

- **Ad Fatigue & Saturated Messaging:** The skincare space is filled with repetitive claims, requiring authentic visual proof and ingredient education to capture attention.
- **Cart Abandonment:** Skincare shoppers frequently compare alternatives and abandon carts before completing payment, necessitating timely retargeting sequences.
- **Unit Economics & Basket Size:** Achieving profitable acquisition on single-item purchases requires encouraging bundles, routine collections, and higher Average Order Value (AOV).

## Objective

- Drive consistent direct-to-consumer purchases across the Shopify store through Meta Ads.
- Build a creative testing framework spotlighting hero products (such as Cold Pressed Rosehip Seed Oil and Eye-Lixir serums).
- Integrate Shopify e-commerce tracking with Meta Pixel and Google Analytics to track complete customer journeys from ad impression to checkout.

## Strategy

1. **Product-Hero Creative Testing:** Segmented top-of-funnel ad creatives into distinct angles: ingredient transparency (cold-pressed formulation benefits), routine pairings (morning vs. evening skincare steps), and unboxing texture demonstrations.
2. **Shopify Funnel Integration:** Connected catalog feeds to dynamic product ads (DPA) and customized product detail landing pages, ensuring smooth transitions from Instagram and Facebook feeds into checkout.
3. **Structured Retargeting Sequences:** Deployed targeted retargeting tiers: engaging recent site visitors with trust indicators and dermatological purity messaging, and re-engaging abandoned cart users with time-sensitive bundle incentives.

## Execution

- **Campaign Architecture:** Managed dedicated prospecting campaigns targeting beauty, clean skincare, and self-care audiences, complemented by narrow custom audience retargeting for active shoppers.
- **Conversion API & GA4 Tracking:** Ensured server-side Meta Conversions API (CAPI) and Google Analytics 4 e-commerce purchase tracking captured add-to-cart, initiated checkout, and purchase events without browser-level drop-offs.
- **Bundle & Cross-Sell Promotions:** Highlighted complementary pairings—such as rosehip seed oil paired with silk beauty accessories—in collection ads to elevate order values.
- **Continuous Creative Iteration:** Scaled winning video and carousel formats while pausing underperforming variants based on add-to-cart velocity and purchase ROAS signals.

## Results

Across the 8-month performance marketing partnership, KishKin established a resilient direct-to-consumer acquisition channel. Meta Ads consistently directed qualified shoppers into the Shopify store, driving steady checkout completions for flagship skincare products and laying the groundwork for sustained repeat-customer value.

## Key Takeaways

- In D2C beauty, creative diversity (texture videos, ingredient breakdowns, customer reviews) is the single biggest factor in preventing ad fatigue.
- Driving bundle purchases rather than standalone single SKUs dramatically improves customer acquisition unit economics.
- Flawless event tracking between Meta and Shopify is required to identify drop-off points between product view and completed purchase.
