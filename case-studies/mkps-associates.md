### Elevating Corporate Governance and Internal Collaboration for a 70-Year Chartered Accountancy Firm

How strategic information architecture, senior partner credentialing, and a secure internal members portal on Wix Studio modernized the digital presence of one of Mumbai's historic accounting practices.

## Project Overview

MKPS & Associates LLP is a premier Indian Chartered Accountancy firm established in 1952. Operating across major commercial hubs with headquarters in Mumbai, the firm provides specialized services across statutory audit, corporate taxation, forensic accounting, banking advisory, and regulatory compliance. In high-stakes financial advisory, digital presence must project institutional integrity, partner depth, and regulatory rigor. Over a 7-month development partnership, Kajal & Co. collaborated with the firm's leadership to design in Figma and implement a robust corporate web platform and secure internal members portal on Wix Studio.

## The Challenge

Redesigning the digital presence of a multi-decade institutional accountancy firm involves specific reputational and operational requirements:

- **Institutional Authority vs. Modern Usability:** The website needed to reflect over seven decades of professional trust without feeling antiquated or text-heavy.
- **Multi-Disciplinary Service Clarity:** The firm represents diverse practice areas (statutory audits for nationalized banks, cross-border corporate tax, forensic investigations); communicating these capabilities clearly to corporate CFOs and board directors was essential.
- **Secure Internal Knowledge Sharing:** Beyond a public-facing website, the firm required a protected internal communication hub where partners, associates, and audit staff across branches could access firm circulars, regulatory updates, and practice documentation securely.

## Objective

- Modernize the firm's corporate web interface, presenting practice areas, partner credentials, and regulatory achievements with executive clarity.
- Design an intuitive navigation hierarchy that enables prospective corporate and institutional clients to review relevant advisory capabilities quickly.
- Build and integrate a secure, login-protected Internal Members Portal within Wix Studio to centralize nationwide team communications and documentation.

## Strategy

1. **Executive-Tier Design System:** Designed a crisp, corporate visual hierarchy in Figma incorporating understated corporate palettes, refined typography, and structured partner profile cards.
2. **Practice Area & Sector Segmentation:** Structured services into clear commercial categories (Assurance, Direct & Indirect Tax, Corporate Advisory, Forensic Services), backing each with regulatory context and industry specializations.
3. **Dual-Layer Architecture (Public & Internal):** Separated public client acquisition pathways from a private, authenticated members area dedicated to internal firm operations and branch coordination.

## Execution

- **Figma Prototyping:** Crafted high-fidelity wireframes and responsive layouts for practice landing pages, partner directories, and corporate governance sections.
- **Wix Studio Development:** Implemented the full design in Wix Studio, ensuring pixel-precise responsiveness across desktop, tablet, and mobile browsers.
- **Secure Members Portal Build:** Configured role-based, password-protected member pages within the platform, enabling the management team to publish internal notices, compliance circulars, and resource guides securely.
- **Corporate Publishing Workflows:** Structured a streamlined CMS for publishing firm updates, regulatory insights (such as RBI circular interpretations), and thought leadership articles without technical complexity.

## Results

Across the 7-month engagement, MKPS & Associates LLP successfully transitioned to a contemporary, highly functional digital home. The new platform accurately reflected the scale and intellectual depth of its 70-year practice, while the integrated internal portal gave the nationwide audit team a private, centralized workspace for daily coordination.

## Key Takeaways

- For established professional service firms, digital credibility rests on partner visibility, clear practice definitions, and institutional heritage.
- Integrating private team portals directly into corporate web platforms eliminates the need for fragmented third-party intranets.
- Clean CMS architecture empowers non-technical administrative teams to keep regulatory insights and firm announcements constantly up to date.
