## Hero

### Modernising Heritage Ayurveda for Digital Direct-to-Consumer Commerce

How responsive Shopify architecture, authentic botanical storytelling, and conversion-focused product pages brought a legacy Ayurvedic brand into modern digital retail.

## Project Overview

Aarshaveda is a heritage Ayurvedic brand with roots stretching back to 1945 (beginning as Kerala Ayurveda Pharmacy Limited in Aluva, Kerala). Built on generational Ayurvedic formulation expertise and modern AYUSH-licensed manufacturing, the brand achieved national acclaim through flagship products like Dasapushpam Baby Oil. To scale its direct-to-consumer footprint and reflect its premium botanical quality, Aarshaveda partnered with Kajal & Co. for a 7-month UX/UI design and Shopify development engagement.

## The Challenge

Translating traditional Ayurvedic heritage into modern digital e-commerce presents specific design problems:

- **Balancing Tradition with Modern E-commerce UX:** Traditional Ayurveda websites often feel either overly clinical or crowded with dense Sanskrit terminology, confusing modern consumers seeking accessible botanical care.
- **Product Detail Clarity:** Customers purchasing premium wellness and baby care formulations require clear information regarding ingredients, clinical purity, usage routines, and safety certifications before buying.
- **Multi-Device Responsiveness:** A large share of wellness shoppers browse on mobile devices while researching formulations on desktop, requiring seamless responsive consistency across all screen viewports.

## Objective

- Redesign and develop an elegant, high-performing Shopify storefront that honours Aarshaveda's 1945 heritage while delivering modern D2C convenience.
- Create structured, conversion-focused Product Detail Pages (PDPs) that highlight ingredient provenance, therapeutic benefits, and customer verification.
- Ensure flawless responsive layouts across desktop, tablet, and mobile devices to support seamless browsing and rapid checkout.

## Strategy

1. **Heritage Meets Purity UI System:** Developed an earthy, elegant visual language balancing warm organic tones with clean editorial typography, positioning Aarshaveda as an authentic, science-backed Ayurvedic authority.
2. **Benefit-First Information Hierarchy:** Re-architected product pages to lead with clear wellness benefits, followed by detailed ingredient breakdowns, certifications, and simple usage instructions.
3. **Cross-Viewport Responsiveness:** Designed adaptive layouts for desktop, tablet, and mobile that retain strong visual balance, readable type, and accessible sticky "Add to Cart" triggers on smaller screens.

## Execution

- **Shopify Theme Architecture:** Developed a fast, lightweight custom Shopify theme eliminating bloated third-party code to ensure rapid load times.
- **Modular PDP Design:** Built flexible modular sections on product pages for ingredient spotlights, customer reviews, safety certifications, and bundle cross-sells.
- **Category Navigation & Discovery:** Reorganized catalog browsing by wellness concern (Baby Care, Wellness, Daily Rituals), reducing time-to-product for new visitors.
- **Checkout Streamlining:** Optimized Shopify checkout flows with clear trust badges, secure payment highlights, and transparent shipping information.

## Results

Across the 7-month engagement, Aarshaveda launched a refreshed digital flagship on Shopify that beautifully bridged its multi-decade healing heritage with contemporary e-commerce standards. The new storefront provided a polished, responsive customer experience that strengthened brand prestige and streamlined product discovery for wellness-conscious families across India.

## Key Takeaways

- In heritage wellness, honoring authenticity builds stronger commercial trust than chasing generic direct-to-consumer design fads.
- Structuring product pages around wellness concerns rather than dense botanical terminology simplifies decision-making for modern buyers.
- Designing for all screen sizes from day one eliminates friction in mobile wellness shopping journeys.
