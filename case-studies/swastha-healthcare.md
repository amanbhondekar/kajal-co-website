### Building Patient-Centric Digital Journeys for Multispecialty Neighbourhood Clinics

How empathetic UX structure, doctor directory credentialing, and intuitive mobile appointment booking unified two specialized healthcare facilities in Bangalore.

## Project Overview

Swastha Healthcare is a respected healthcare provider operating two specialized neighbourhood clinics in Padmanabhanagar, Bangalore: Swastha Fertility Centre (focusing on Obstetrics, Gynaecology, and advanced IVF/IUI care) and Swastha Healthcare (specializing in Pulmonology, Respiratory Care, and Internal Medicine). Backed by over 25 years of clinical excellence and a legacy of delivering over 12,000 healthy babies, the practice required a consolidated, modern digital platform that communicated medical authority while maintaining warm, patient-first accessibility. Over a 5-month engagement, Kajal & Co. planned, designed in Figma, and developed a custom WordPress web experience.

## The Challenge

Designing a multispecialty healthcare web platform involves strict empathy and informational requirements:

- **Dual-Speciality Complexity:** The practice brings together two distinct medical branches under one umbrella brand: delicate, emotionally sensitive reproductive care and fertility treatments, and diagnostic pulmonology and respiratory medicine. The digital experience needed to guide patients clearly to the right clinic without cross-specialty confusion.
- **Patient Anxiety & Trust Barriers:** Patients seeking fertility care or chronic respiratory support often feel anxious and vulnerable. The interface required warm, reassuring visual design, transparent doctor credentials, and clear clinical outcomes (such as verified clinical success rates and delivery milestones).
- **Mobile-First Urgent Booking:** A vast majority of patients research symptoms and schedule doctor consultations directly on mobile phones, making clunky, slow forms an immediate barrier to care.

## Objective

- Design a cohesive, patient-friendly UX/UI system in Figma reflecting clinical excellence and human compassion.
- Structure intuitive navigation allowing patients to seamlessly explore treatments, doctor profiles, and clinic locations in Padmanabhanagar.
- Develop a fast, responsive WordPress platform featuring streamlined appointment booking and direct WhatsApp/phone clinic contact pathways.

## Strategy

1. **Dual-Clinic Unified Information Architecture:** Created an intuitive bifurcated structure on the homepage allowing patients to choose their care pathway immediately: "Swastha Fertility Centre" (Obstetrics, Gynaecology, IVF/IUI) or "Swastha Healthcare Clinic" (Pulmonology, Respiratory Care, General Medicine).
2. **Physician Authority & Care Transparency:** Designed comprehensive doctor directory profiles spotlighting qualifications, clinical experience, specializations, and patient care philosophies to establish immediate rapport.
3. **Frictionless Mobile Appointment Booking:** Implemented prominent, sticky "Book an Appointment" and direct call triggers across every page, allowing patients to schedule visits in seconds.

## Execution

- **Figma Design System:** Crafted empathetic visual guidelines incorporating calming healthcare palettes, accessible typography scales, custom medical specialty iconography, and responsive mobile layouts.
- **Custom WordPress Development:** Built a lightweight, secure WordPress theme adhering to medical accessibility standards (WCAG-aligned contrast and readability), avoiding bloated plugins to ensure instant loading.
- **Specialty & Condition Treatment Guides:** Built dedicated condition guides explaining common diagnoses (PCOS, high-risk pregnancy, asthma, allergic bronchitis) in clear, patient-friendly language alongside relevant treatment protocols.
- **Clinic Proximity & Directions Integration:** Embedded interactive maps, neighborhood transit details, and contact numbers for both Padmanabhanagar clinic locations, ensuring smooth physical arrival.

## Results

Across the 5-month engagement, Swastha Healthcare launched a modern, compassionate web platform that successfully unified its multispecialty practice. The new site demystified complex fertility and respiratory treatments for prospective patients, providing a trustworthy, mobile-optimized gateway to quality healthcare for families across Bangalore.

## Key Takeaways

- In healthcare web design, emotional tone and clarity of information directly reduce patient anxiety before their initial consultation.
- Clearly delineating distinct medical specialities on the homepage prevents patient disorientation and guides visitors to appropriate doctors faster.
- Fast, accessible mobile appointment pathways are essential; patients seeking care prioritize convenience and direct communication.
