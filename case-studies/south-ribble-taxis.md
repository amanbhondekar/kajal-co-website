### Dominating Local Ride Intent and Airport Transfer Searches

## Project Overview

South Ribble Taxis is a private hire and taxi dispatch firm operating across the borough of South Ribble, Lancashire—encompassing communities including Leyland, Bamber Bridge, Penwortham, Lostock Hall, and Walton-le-Dale. In local private hire transportation, search engine optimization is predominantly mobile, immediate, and location-driven. Over an 8-month engagement, Kajal & Co. executed a comprehensive local SEO roadmap to capture both immediate passenger dispatch queries and high-margin pre-booked airport transfers to Manchester and Liverpool airports.

## The Challenge

Local taxi and transport search involves distinct technical and behavioral dynamics:

- **Immediate Mobile Intent:** When passengers search for a local taxi, over 80% do so from a smartphone requiring an instantaneous phone call connection rather than form fills.
- **Borough-Wide Geographic Dispersion:** The South Ribble council area contains multiple distinct towns and villages; a single generic homepage cannot effectively rank for town-specific queries like "taxi in Bamber Bridge" or "Penwortham cabs".
- **High-Margin Airport Transfers vs. Short Hops:** Short local runs keep drivers moving, but pre-booked airport transfers provide predictable, high-value revenue that requires dedicated booking visibility.

## Objective

- Secure top positions in Google's local map pack for urgent, proximity-based taxi queries across the borough.
- Build dedicated service-area landing pages for every key locality in South Ribble.
- Optimize the website for mobile passengers with persistent click-to-call features and clear airport transfer quote structures.

## Strategy

1. **Town-Specific Service-Area Pages:** Created structured landing pages for key commercial hubs (Leyland, Bamber Bridge, Penwortham, Lostock Hall, Hutton, Walton-le-Dale), each optimized for local postal codes and transit landmarks (train stations, hospital transfers).
2. **Dedicated Airport Transfer Pages:** Developed high-intent landing pages for Manchester Airport and Liverpool John Lennon Airport transfers, outlining fixed fares, flight tracking, and vehicle capacity (saloon, estate, 8-seater minibuses).
3. **Mobile Click-to-Call Prioritization:** Re-engineered the mobile user experience with sticky "Call Dispatch" headers and prominent 1-tap dialling buttons, reducing friction between search arrival and driver booking.

## Execution

- **Local Business & Schema Markup:** Deployed LocalBusiness, TaxiService, and GeoCoordinates schema markup across all service pages to assist search engines in understanding exact operational boundaries.
- **Google Business Profile Alignment:** Re-categorized the Google Business Profile, aligned Primary and Secondary categories (Taxi Service, Airport Shuttle Service), and maintained verified local business citations across UK directories.
- **Page Speed & Mobile Performance:** Minimized code weight and optimized assets to ensure instantaneous page load on cellular mobile networks.
- **Conversion Tracking:** Set up call-click tracking and transfer booking form tracking in Google Analytics to measure mobile passenger volume.

## Results

Across the 8-month optimization period, South Ribble Taxis established a commanding local search footprint across the borough. The company achieved top-tier placement for local town queries and airport transfer searches, translating mobile search queries directly into daily telephone dispatch bookings and scheduled airport runs.

## Key Takeaways

- In transportation SEO, mobile call friction is the primary conversion barrier; every extra tap reduces dispatch volume.
- Building distinct service-area pages for individual towns captures granular local searches that generic borough-wide homepages miss.
- Pre-booked airport transfer pages capture high-value revenue that buffers against daily dispatch fluctuation.
