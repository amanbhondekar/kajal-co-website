Cookie Policy

Last updated 15 October 2026

This Cookie Policy explains how Kajal & Co. may use cookies and similar technologies on its website.

1. What Are Cookies?

Cookies are small text files placed on your device when you visit a website.

They can help websites:

  • Remember settings
  • Maintain basic functionality
  • Understand how visitors use a website
  • Measure website performance
  • Improve user experience
  • Support certain marketing or advertising functions

Similar technologies may include pixels, tags, scripts, local storage, and related tracking mechanisms.

2. How Kajal & Co. Uses Cookies

Kajal & Co. may use cookies and similar technologies for the following purposes:

Essential technologies

These may be required for the website to operate properly, maintain security, or support basic functionality.

Analytics technologies

Analytics technologies may be introduced after the website is hosted to understand traffic, page performance, visitor behaviour, and website usage.

Preference technologies

Where used, these technologies may remember choices or settings to improve the user experience.

Marketing technologies

Marketing or advertising technologies may be used in the future where required to measure campaigns, understand advertising performance, or support remarketing.

Any such technologies will be reviewed and configured in accordance with applicable legal requirements.

3. Third-Party Cookies

Some technologies may be provided by third-party services used by Kajal & Co.

Depending on the website configuration, these may include services related to:

  • Website hosting
  • Analytics
  • Advertising
  • Scheduling
  • Embedded media
  • Forms
  • Social media
  • Security

Third-party providers may place or access cookies according to their own policies.

4. Current Analytics Configuration

Kajal & Co.’s final analytics and tracking configuration will be determined when the website is hosted.

Because the specific analytics providers are not yet fixed, this policy intentionally describes the categories and purposes of tracking rather than naming tools that may not ultimately be used.

Once analytics or marketing tracking is activated, this policy should be updated to identify the relevant services and their purposes.

5. Managing Cookies

You can manage or delete cookies through your browser settings.

Most browsers allow you to:

  • View stored cookies
  • Delete cookies
  • Block cookies
  • Block third-party cookies
  • Receive warnings before cookies are stored

Disabling certain cookies may affect website functionality.

Where legally required, Kajal & Co. may also provide additional cookie controls on the website.

6. Changes to This Cookie Policy

Kajal & Co. may update this Cookie Policy when tracking technologies or website functionality change.

The updated version will be published on this page with a revised "Last updated" date.
