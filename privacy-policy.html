Privacy Policy

Last updated 15 October 2026

Kajal & Co. respects your privacy and is committed to handling personal information responsibly.

This Privacy Policy explains what information we collect through our website, why we collect it, how we use it, where it may be stored, when it may be shared, and the choices available to you.

This policy applies to information collected from visitors, prospective clients, and people who contact Kajal & Co. through the website.

It does not cover confidential information, website analytics, advertising data, campaign data, SEO data, or other information processed as part of a client engagement. Those activities are governed by the relevant client agreement and applicable service terms.

1. Information We Collect

When you use the website or contact us, we may collect information such as:

  • Your name
  • Email address
  • Phone or WhatsApp number, if you choose to provide it
  • Country or country code, where collected through the contact form
  • The service you are interested in
  • Information contained in your enquiry or message
  • Information you voluntarily provide when communicating with us

We only ask for information that is reasonably relevant to responding to your enquiry or providing the requested service information.

2. How We Collect Information

Information may be collected when you:

  • Submit the website contact form
  • Email Kajal & Co.
  • Contact us through other communication channels linked from the website
  • Book or request a discovery call through a scheduling link, if available

The website contact form sends enquiry information to email and may also store submitted information in Google Sheets for enquiry management and follow-up.

3. How We Use Your Information

We may use your information to:

  • Respond to enquiries and requests
  • Understand your business requirements
  • Arrange discovery calls or consultations
  • Follow up regarding an enquiry you submitted
  • Provide information about Kajal & Co.’s services
  • Manage and organise incoming leads
  • Improve our website, services, and enquiry experience
  • Send email marketing where you have separately opted in or where otherwise permitted by applicable law
  • Maintain records necessary for legitimate business, legal, accounting, or security purposes

We do not use information collected through the website to create unrelated marketing profiles about you.

4. Email Follow-Up and Marketing

If you contact Kajal & Co., we may contact you by email to respond to your enquiry, clarify requirements, discuss next steps, or follow up on the service you requested.

Marketing emails are separate from enquiry-related communication. Where required, we will obtain appropriate consent before sending promotional or marketing communications.

You can unsubscribe from marketing emails at any time by using the unsubscribe mechanism included in the email or by contacting us.

Unsubscribing from marketing communication does not prevent us from sending necessary responses to an active enquiry or service-related communication.

5. Google Sheets and Email

Information submitted through the website contact form may be transmitted to Kajal & Co.’s email and stored in Google Sheets for lead and enquiry management.

Google is a third-party service provider. Information stored or processed through Google’s services may be subject to Google’s own terms and privacy practices.

Kajal & Co. uses reasonable administrative and technical measures to limit access to enquiry information to people who need it for legitimate business purposes.

6. Discovery Calls and Scheduling

Kajal & Co. may use a third-party scheduling service to allow visitors to book discovery calls.

Our recommended scheduling setup is Cal.com, connected to Google Calendar and, where appropriate, Google Meet. Cal.com supports calendar availability, conflict checking, booking forms, reminders, and Google Meet integrations.

If you use a scheduling link, the information you submit through that service may be processed by the scheduling provider under its own privacy policy and terms.

You should review the provider’s privacy information before submitting information through a third-party booking page.

7. Analytics and Tracking

Kajal & Co. may use analytics or similar technologies after the website is hosted to understand website traffic, identify usability issues, measure website performance, and improve the user experience.

The specific analytics and tracking services may change over time. When such services are enabled, Kajal & Co. will update this Privacy Policy and Cookie Policy as appropriate to identify the relevant categories and purposes of tracking.

Analytics or marketing technologies will not be used in a way that is inconsistent with applicable law.

8. Cookies

The website may use cookies and similar technologies.

Some cookies may be necessary for the website to function correctly. Other cookies may be used for analytics, performance measurement, preferences, or marketing if those technologies are introduced.

You can manage cookies through your browser settings. Depending on the technologies used on the website, additional cookie controls may also be provided.

For more information, see the Cookie Policy.

9. How We Share Information

Kajal & Co. does not sell your personal information.

Information may be shared with service providers where reasonably necessary to operate the website or respond to your enquiry, including providers used for:

  • Email
  • Form processing
  • Google Sheets or related productivity services
  • Website hosting
  • Scheduling and calendar management
  • Analytics or tracking, if enabled
  • Security, maintenance, or technical support

We may also disclose information where required by law, legal process, regulatory requirements, or to protect the rights, property, security, or legitimate interests of Kajal & Co. or others.

10. Data Retention

We retain enquiry information for as long as reasonably necessary to:

  • Respond to and manage the enquiry
  • Maintain appropriate business records
  • Follow up on potential business opportunities
  • Meet legal, accounting, tax, security, or dispute-resolution requirements

When information is no longer reasonably required, we may delete it or securely anonymise it.

Retention periods may vary depending on the type of information and the reason it was collected.

11. Data Security

Kajal & Co. takes reasonable measures to protect personal information against unauthorised access, misuse, loss, alteration, or disclosure.

However, no website, email system, cloud service, or method of transmission over the internet can be guaranteed to be completely secure.

You should avoid sending passwords, payment credentials, highly sensitive personal information, or confidential information through the website contact form.

12. Third-Party Links

The website may contain links to third-party websites, tools, platforms, social networks, scheduling services, or other resources.

Kajal & Co. is not responsible for the privacy practices, security, content, or policies of third-party websites.

Your use of those services is governed by their own terms and privacy policies.

Depending on applicable law, you may have rights relating to your personal information, including the ability to request access to, correction of, or deletion of information, withdraw consent where processing is based on consent, or raise a concern about how your information is handled.

Kajal & Co. does not knowingly seek to collect personal information from children through the website.
