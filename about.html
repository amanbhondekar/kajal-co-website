The story

Why I started Kajal & Co.

I’m Kajal Andani, a digital marketer, content creator, and educator. I’ve generated ₹1 Cr+ in revenue for clients across 60+ brands, and trained 400+ people in digital marketing.

I started out in-house, then freelanced across a dozen categories before this became a studio. What I learned is that most marketing problems are not creative problems. A brand with weak sales rarely needs a bolder campaign; it needs a page that answers the obvious objection, a search result where buyers are already looking, or an ad account that can tell profitable spend from noise.

So we work backwards from the number that matters and pick the smallest piece of work that moves it. Sometimes that is a website. Often it is not. Being honest about which is the part most agencies skip, and it is the reason clients stay with us past the first quarter.
