Who do you work with?
Brands that sell high-consideration products or services, where customers research before they buy.
What happens on the discovery call?
We look at your website, search presence, or ad account — you choose one — and talk through the first three things we’d change.
What does it cost to work with you?
Websites start at ₹1,20,000. SEO and performance marketing run as monthly retainers from ₹45,000, with ad spend paid directly to the platforms. The call tells you which setup fits before you commit to anything.
How fast can you start?
Usually one to two weeks from agreeing scope. If something is genuinely on fire we can often start sooner.
Do I need all three services?
No. Most clients start with one and add others when it makes sense.
How long until I see results?
Ads can show signal within weeks. SEO typically compounds over 3–6 months. A new website goes live in 4–8 weeks depending on size.
Why work with Kajal & Co. instead of hiring in-house or a bigger agency?
Hiring in-house takes time and budget, and one person rarely covers design, search, and paid well. Large agencies often pass you between account managers. With us you get specialists in all three, with one person accountable for the result.
What happens after I get in touch?
We read every enquiry ourselves. If it’s a fit we’ll set up a call within two working days and come to it with a point of view. If it isn’t, we’ll say so and point you to what to do instead.
