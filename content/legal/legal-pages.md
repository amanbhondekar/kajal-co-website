## 1. What Are Cookies?

Cookies are small text files placed on your device when you visit a
website.

They can help websites:

-   Remember settings
-   Maintain basic functionality
-   Understand how visitors use a website
-   Measure website performance
-   Improve user experience
-   Support certain marketing or advertising functions

Similar technologies may include pixels, tags, scripts, local storage,
and related tracking mechanisms.

## 2. How Kajal & Co. Uses Cookies

Kajal & Co. may use cookies and similar technologies for the following
purposes:

### Essential technologies

These may be required for the website to operate properly, maintain
security, or support basic functionality.

### Analytics technologies

Analytics technologies may be introduced after the website is hosted to
understand traffic, page performance, visitor behaviour, and website
usage.

### Preference technologies

Where used, these technologies may remember choices or settings to
improve the user experience.

### Marketing technologies

Marketing or advertising technologies may be used in the future where
required to measure campaigns, understand advertising performance, or
support remarketing.

Any such technologies will be reviewed and configured in accordance with
applicable legal requirements.

## 3. Third-Party Cookies

## 4. Current Analytics Configuration

## Footer

## Contact Form

The website contact form should clearly identify the information being
collected and should avoid collecting unnecessary sensitive information.

For marketing emails, use a separate, unticked consent checkbox rather
than treating an enquiry submission as blanket consent to marketing.

Recommended wording:

> **I'd like to receive occasional marketing emails from Kajal & Co. I
> can unsubscribe at any time.**

## Discovery Call

**Recommended setup: Cal.com + Google Calendar + Google Meet.**

Cal.com is a strong fit if Kajal & Co. wants a more customizable booking
experience and control over the booking flow. It supports calendar
syncing, availability management, booking questions, reminders, and
Google Meet integrations.

Calendly is the stronger alternative if simplicity and broad familiarity
are the priority.

Google Calendar + Google Meet alone can handle meetings, but they are
not as suitable as a dedicated booking layer for a public website.
